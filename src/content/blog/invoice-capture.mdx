## The problem with manual capture

Someone opens each PDF or photo, reads it and types the same details into the accounts. It's slow, and the mistakes are small and easy to miss: a transposed number, the wrong VAT amount, a duplicate payment.

## How it works

<Schedule>
  <When label="Collect">Invoices arrive in one inbox or folder, not scattered across people.</When>
  <When label="Read">AI extracts supplier, invoice number, date, totals, VAT and line items.</When>
  <When label="Check">Rules flag anything unusual: new suppliers, amounts above a limit, possible duplicates.</When>
  <When label="Post">Clean invoices are prepared for your accounting system. Flagged ones go to a person.</When>
</Schedule>

<Checklist>
