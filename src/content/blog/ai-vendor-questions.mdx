<ShortAnswer>
Before you sign with any AI vendor, ask five questions: **where does our data go**, **is it used for training**, **what happens when we leave**, **what does it really cost at our size**, and **who do we call when it breaks**. Clear answers are a good sign. Vague ones are your answer.
</ShortAnswer>

## Why these five

AI products are easy to demo and hard to compare. The demo shows the best case. The contract decides what happens in every other case. These questions move the conversation from features to the things that cost money later.

## The questions

<NumberedList>
  <NumberedItem title="Where does our data go?">Which country it's stored and processed in, and which other companies (sub-processors) touch it. This matters under POPIA.</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Is our data used to train your models?">Get the answer in writing, for the plan you're actually buying.</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="What happens when we leave?">Can you export your data and configuration in a usable format? How quickly is it deleted?</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="What will it cost at our size?">Per-user, per-use and add-on pricing can add up. Ask for a quote based on your real numbers, including year two.</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Who do we call when it breaks?">Support hours, response times and whether there's anyone in a South African time zone.</NumberedItem>
</NumberedList>
