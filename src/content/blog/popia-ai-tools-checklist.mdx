## Why POPIA matters for AI tools

The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) applies whenever your business processes personal information: names, ID numbers, contact details, health or financial information about clients, staff or suppliers. Pasting that information into an AI tool is processing it. So is uploading a spreadsheet or connecting a tool to your inbox.

None of that is forbidden. It just needs the same care you'd give any other system that holds client data.

## The checklist

<NumberedList>
  <NumberedItem title="What personal information would go in?">List the actual types: client names, ID numbers, medical details, salaries. Be specific.</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Do we need it to?">Often the task works just as well with names and numbers removed. Use the minimum.</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Is it for the purpose we collected it for?">Information collected to deliver a service shouldn't quietly be used for something unrelated.</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Where is the data stored and processed?">Many AI tools process data outside South Africa. POPIA has specific conditions for cross-border transfers.</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Does the provider train on our data?">Check the plan you're on. Business plans often differ from free and personal ones.</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Is there a written agreement?">A provider processing personal information for you is an operator under POPIA, and you need a contract that covers security.</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Who in the business can use it?">Access should match job roles, the same as any other system.</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="How long is information kept?">Know the tool's retention settings and set them deliberately.</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="What happens if something goes wrong?">POPIA requires you to notify the Information Regulator and affected people of a security compromise. Know who would do that.</NumberedItem>
  <NumberedItem title="Who is accountable?">Name the person. In most small businesses that's your Information Officer, often the owner.</NumberedItem>
</NumberedList>
